What Gross Salary Gives You $55,000 Take-Home in Texas?

To take home $55,000 per year in Texas in 2026, you need a gross salary of approximately $65,737.

That means $10,737 — 16.3% of your gross pay — goes to taxes before it reaches your bank account. Here is exactly where it goes:

How Texas taxes shape this number

Texas has no state income tax, so the gap between gross and net comes entirely from federal income tax and FICA.

At this income your marginal combined rate is about 22% — each additional dollar of raise leaves you roughly 78¢. Your effective (average) rate is lower, 16.3%, because the first dollars you earn are taxed at lower brackets (and the standard deduction isn't taxed at all).

Frequently asked

Is $65,737 before or after 401(k) contributions?

Before. This estimate assumes no pre-tax deductions. Every dollar you put into a traditional 401(k) or pre-tax health premiums lowers your taxable income, so with benefits you would need a somewhat higher gross to still clear $55,000 in cash take-home.

What is the marginal tax rate at this income in Texas?

At $65,737 gross, earning one more dollar keeps you about 78 cents after federal, FICA and Texas taxes — an effective marginal rate of roughly 22%.

Does this include local or city taxes?

No. Texas has no general local wage income taxes, so this estimate is close to complete.
