Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

NetToGross.com is a free payroll calculator. This page explains what happens to information when you use it. It is written to be short and accurate rather than exhaustive.

What we do not collect

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Salary figures, tax rates and any other numbers you type are processed on your own device and are never transmitted to us or stored on any server. We have no account system, no login, and no database of user submissions. If you close the tab, the numbers are gone.

What is collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs: IP address, browser type, referring page, and the pages requested. We use aggregate traffic analytics to understand which pages are used. We do not attempt to identify individual visitors.

Cookies and third-party advertising

We display advertising served by Google AdSense and may participate in affiliate programs. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a consent notice before personalised advertising cookies are set, in line with Google's EU user consent policy.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links to payroll software providers are affiliate links. If you sign up through one, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. These links are marked as sponsored. Commission does not influence what the calculator outputs.

Children

This site is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings and continue to use the calculator normally — it does not depend on cookies to function. If you are a California or EEA resident and wish to make a data request, contact us at the address below; because we hold no personal records, most requests can be answered immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
